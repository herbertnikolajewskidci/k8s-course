## Success Criteria

1. **Terminal Fluency**: Immediate recall of `kubectl` generators,
   dry-runs (`--dry-run=client -o yaml`), JSONPath filters, and custom columns.
2. **Speed & Accuracy**: Solving standard CKA tasks in <= 4–5 minutes each.
3. **Troubleshooting Mastery (30%)**: Systematic diagnosis of broken worker
   nodes, systemd/kubelet failures, control-plane misconfigurations, and
   NetworkPolicies.
4. **Wissensnetz Anchor**: Deep mental models of internal Kubernetes
   architecture using Birkenbihl techniques (KaWa, metaphors).
